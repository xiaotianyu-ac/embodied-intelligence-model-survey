# Research protocol

## Questions

What model roles form an embodied feedback loop? Which representative architectures changed learning or execution? How do the models relate through explicit reuse, series evolution, method combination, and system complementarity? What do evaluation and product evidence support, and what remains unresolved?

## Scope

Robotic manipulation, language-conditioned action, perception/representation, hierarchical planning, predictive world models, navigation, whole-body/humanoid control, data, evaluation, and product maturity. Foundational work from 2018 onward and updates verified through 2026-09-11. Earlier imitation/control ideas appear as background. Driving world models are an adjacent comparison; an exhaustive driving, SLAM, hardware, tactile-sensor or classical-control survey is excluded.

## Selection and evidence

Use original papers, author project pages, official repositories/model cards, vendor technical disclosures, and customer confirmations. Include seminal mechanisms, open reference implementations, materially different training/action designs, and recent verified updates. Exclude unsupported rumors, duplicate secondary summaries, purely promotional superiority claims, and unrelated systems with similar names. Retain versions where a change explains a technical relationship.

Queries and selection notes are preserved by research module and in the combined search log. This is a route-based technical scoping review, not a registered systematic review. Search-result or exclusion counts were not exhaustively recorded and are not fabricated. Source URL deduplication does not equate sources with independent research studies.

## Extraction

For each card: name/version, year, role/family, problem when explicit, inputs, architecture, action representation, objective, data, inference/feedback, contribution, limitations, openness, and source IDs. For each edge: endpoints, relationship type, explicit/analytical status, explanation, and evidence. For products: exact model/system, stage, supporting source, and limitation.

## Comparison rules

Never infer weight inheritance from similar modules. Keep tasks, data visibility, embodiment, action space, rollout horizon, compute, sensor setup, adaptation, trials, and checkpoint dates attached to performance claims. Separate paper results, vendor reports, customer confirmation, and analytical recommendations. Preserve undisclosed fields. No robot evaluation or training is claimed to have been run.

## Stop rule and updates

Stop after all scope roles have representative evidence, key relationships are explained, version conflicts are bounded, and unresolved limitations are explicit. New releases can be added through the documented update workflow; an update must revise both languages, graphs, catalogs, and validation artifacts together.
