# Coverage map / 范围覆盖

The survey contains 77 cards, including distinct versions and methods, 80 documented relationship edges, 155 unique source URLs, and 11 product organizations. These are catalog counts, not PRISMA study counts or a claim of exhaustive literature recall.

| Scope | Representative coverage | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Imitation and action distributions | ACT, Diffusion Policy, FAST, OFT | Methods are not all foundation models |
| VLA pretraining and adaptation | RT, RT-X, Octo, OpenVLA, PI, GR00T, Gemini | Versions and actual released assets are separated |
| Compact and cross-embodiment policies | SmolVLA, RDT, X-VLA, UniVLA, LingBot | Same-name papers have separate author/paper identities |
| Latent planning and open reasoning-action | GO-1, MolmoAct2 | Intermediate plans require robot grounding |
| Runtime-aware action | RTC, Xiaomi-Robotics-0 | Throughput and feedback latency are different |
| Representation and geometry | R3M, VC-1, PerAct, RVT, 3D Diffuser Actor | An encoder is not a policy |
| Semantic planning | SayCan, PaLM-E, VIMA, VoxPoser, ER | Text planning does not establish torque control |
| World models | Dreamer, UniSim, Genie, GR, JEPA, Cosmos, GAIA | Video generation and robot planning have distinct evidence |
| Full-body and imitation control | DeepMimic, AMP/ASE, PHC, HumanPlus, H2O, SONIC | Simulated characters are not real-robot results |
| Navigation and memory | ViNT, NoMaD, GOAT | GOAT and GOAT-Bench are distinct |
| Data and feedback | OXE, Bridge, DROID, RoboMIND, AgiBot, UMI, HIL-SERL | Dataset overlap and human cost are retained |
| Evaluation and deployment | LIBERO, CALVIN, SIMPLER, RoboTwin, RMBench, RoboCasa, BEHAVIOR, products | No single cross-protocol leaderboard |

Adjacent or non-exhaustive topics: classic SLAM/control, autonomous-driving perception, robot hardware design, tactile-model taxonomies, manipulation-specific mechanics, medical robotics, aerial/underwater robots, formal safety certification, and every newly released VLA. These can form separate follow-up surveys rather than being silently claimed complete here.

The reports discuss five forward challenges and provide an explicit evaluation design. The repository does not execute robot training, benchmark rollouts, hardware measurements, or procurement validation.
