# Research modules / 专题材料

`vla/`, `world/`, `wholebody/`, and `synthesis/` retain the checked bilingual source text and structured research records. Their local source IDs begin V, W, H, and R. The assembler deduplicates exact canonical URLs and maps those IDs to S-series source IDs and numbered report footnotes through `records/artifacts/source-aliases.json`.

The canonical reports and comparison tables are in `records/artifacts/`. Model cards and relationship records add structured detail beyond the prose. Search logs record actual queries and selection notes; they do not fabricate total search-result counts or exhaustive screening statistics.
