# Attribution and reuse

This repository continues the research-workspace organization of [xiaotianyu-ac/agent-memory-survey](https://github.com/xiaotianyu-ac/agent-memory-survey), which adapted the intent/records/artifacts structure from [CodeGandee/dualdecoder-kvcache-survey](https://github.com/CodeGandee/dualdecoder-kvcache-survey). It is a new repository, not a fork of the embodied models discussed.

Topic-specific prose, catalogs, synthesis, and diagrams were prepared for this survey. PDF construction follows the prior survey's Markdown-to-ReportLab approach with a new builder. No original robotics model code, pretrained weights, datasets, or third-party paper PDFs are bundled.

Source links and bibliographic records identify the original authors. Their rights and licenses are unaffected. A public repository, downloadable weights, available training code, and permission for commercial use are distinct properties; consult the relevant original license before reuse.
